  - **Gameplay:** Offers 5 distinct trivia roads across 7 questions (700 points max: Perfect `100`, Excellent `80`, Great `60`, Good `30`, Weak `10`). ## Daily Geography & World Map Quizzes

- **[Farflung — Free Daily & Unlimited Map Quiz on playmixy.com](https://playmixy.com/games/farflung)**
  - **Gameplay:** Each round gives you 5 geographic clues (e.g., *"A country that uses the East Caribbean dollar"* or countries sharing a specific latitude, border, or feature). Multiple countries can be right, and each accepted country belongs to a rarity tier:
    - **Common:** `40 km`
    - **Uncommon:** `55 km`
    - **Rare:** `70 km`
    - **Elusive:** `85 km`

- **[GeoGrid (`geogridgame.com`)](https://www.geogridgame.com/)**
  - **Gameplay:** Fill a 3×3 grid where every cell sits at the intersection of two geographic or demographic clues. You can use each country only once per board, and rarer valid countries earn a better (lower) rarity score.

  - **Gameplay:** Guess the mystery country or territory from its outline in up to 6 attempts. Each miss reveals the distance, compass direction, and proximity percentage.

  - **Gameplay:** Name a chain of contiguous countries to travel overland from a starting country to a destination in as few steps as possible.

## Crowd & Popular-Answer Trivia

Games that flip the rare-answer formula upside down: instead of hunting for an obscure edge case, you win by predicting **what the largest number of players will type first**.

- **[Noillirk — Free Daily & Unlimited Crowd Trivia on playmixy.com](https://playmixy.com/games/noillirk)**
  - **Gameplay:** Literally *"Krillion"* spelled backwards. Across 8 prompts with a 20-second clock, you type the single answer you expect to top the crowd's board (in the `Noillirk.today` edition):
    - **1st place on board:** `100 pts` (+5,000 m altitude)
    - **2nd or 3rd place:** `75 pts`
    - **4th or 5th place:** `50 pts`
    - **6th to 10th place:** `25 pts`
    - **Accepted off the board:** `10 pts` (perfect 8-question climb = **800 points / 40,000 metres**)
  - **Why play on playmixy.com:** Instant browser play with no login, automatic player height adjustment, side-by-side comparison between `Noillirk.today` (8 prompts, 20s) and `Noillirk.com / Crowdict` (10 prompts, 30s), plus both Daily and free Unlimited modes.
- **[Wonderhop — Crowd Mode on playmixy.com](https://playmixy.com/games/wonderhop)**
  - **Gameplay:** Choose the **Crowd (↑ Up)** road in Wonderhop for 7 prompts (30 seconds each) that reward the most popular valid answer, or choose **Compass** to alternate between popular (*Crowd*), rare (*Deep*), earliest (*Oldest*), and most recent (*Newest*) answers within the same game.
